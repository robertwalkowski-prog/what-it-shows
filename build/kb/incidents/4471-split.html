How each report is placed

  1. Parser. A day-first tenant reports through app or email, or through a booking-v2 form (hint DD/MM/YYYY). The shared parser reads month first, so day and month swap.
  2. Form hint. A tenant on booking-v3 reports through form. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field and reads the answer day first. People type what the hint says. A parser fix does not change the form.
  3. One-day move. The desk already listed five reports in desk-notes.json as a one-day move: the date shifts by a day and depends on where the reader is (“Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday”). That is time zone handling, not a swap. The desk files them under parser by tenant and footnotes them; here they also have their own row and ticket.

The cause comes from the tenant (locale and booking form), as causes.ts says. The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser and disagrees with the tenant on dozens of these reports, so it is shown only for comparison.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on subject line. “Wrong date”, “Booking on the wrong date” and “Month and day swapped?” fit all three bugs, so all three landed in one ticket. The decision accepted that cost for different subjects about one bug; it did not cover one subject about different bugs. That needs a new decision record, not an edit to 0001.

What the desk does now

  • New day/month swaps after 5 October: a regression of the parser fix. Open a new ticket.
  • booking-v3 is retired: a form hint report means a tenant still runs it. Move them to a supported form.
  • Route one-day shifts to the time zone ticket. Ask the reporter where each attendee is.
